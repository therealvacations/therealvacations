Questions? We've Got Answers

How do deposits and payment plans work?

Each trip or quote shows the payment options available for that specific booking. TRV deposits or installment plans appear only when configured in Admin; otherwise the checkout will show the applicable pay-in-full or eligible Stripe payment options.

How does the spin wheel discount work?

Spin & Save uses TRV's live prize wheel. The server issues the result, and eligible prize codes are emailed after you opt in. Codes are subject to expiration, trip restrictions, inventory, and availability.

Can I travel solo or do I need a group?

Come solo! Many of our travelers come alone and leave with lifelong connections. That's the magic of group travel.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation, change, and refund terms vary by trip, package, supplier, and rate. The applicable terms are shown with the trip or quote before payment and are included with your booking confirmation.

How do I get notified when new trips open?

Create a free TRV account and opt in to TRV updates, then check the Trips page for current experiences and any promotional offers that are actually available.
