Healthy: required configuration present, Stripe charges/payouts enabled, cron jobs active with recent success, email failures at zero or understood, and Stripe webhook events continuing to arrive when payments occur.

Needs attention: missing configuration, disabled Stripe payments/payouts, failed cron run, repeated email delivery errors, or payment activity without matching webhook events.
